Pre-writing · Grammar

How do we write a diary?

‘I’

1st person singular

Write as the character — I, not he or she.

-ed

Past tense

Write about what already happened today.

He gives the beggar a coin. → I gave the beggar a coin.
"Look at the Baron's diary again. Does he say 'he' or 'I'? — 'I'. And is it present or past? — Past." "So in your diary: use 'I', and use the past tense." Example: if I am Hughie — "I gave the beggar a coin."
