Today’s Activity

Writing a Diary

[Pre-writing · 5m] Hand out the worksheets. "Today's activity is writing a diary — just like the Baron, but as one of the characters in the story."

Writing a Diary

We will…

1

choose a scene and a character from the scene.

2

write a diary as the character.

3

read your partner’s diary and give a comment.

"First, you choose a scene and a character. Then you write a short diary as that character — in English, three to five sentences. Finally, you read your partner's diary and write a comment."

Pre-writing 1 · Components

Components

Who

‘Baron’s’ diary

What

Where

When

today (wedding day)

Impression

“It was a wedding present for somebody who deserved it!”

Elaboration

more details

Analyze the Baron's diary together, one component at a time: "Whose diary is it? — The Baron's. What did he do? — He gave a speech. Where? — At Hughie's wedding. When? — Today, the wedding day. How did he feel? — 'It was a wedding present for somebody who deserved it!'" "And you can add more details — elaboration."

Pre-writing 1 · Grammar

Grammar

“Today I gave a speech
at Hughie Erskine’s wedding.”

→

In first person singular

→

In past tense

"Look at this sentence. Does the Baron say 'he' or 'I'? — 'I'. First person singular." "Is 'gave' present or past? — Past tense." "So when you write your diary, use 'I' and the past tense."
