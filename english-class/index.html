‘Lesson’

"Most stories have five steps. This is called the plot structure." Exposition — we meet the characters and the setting. Rising action — a problem starts and grows. Crisis — things get worse; tension is high. Climax — the most exciting moment, the turning point. Resolution — the problem is solved; the story ends. "And after the story, we can find a lesson — what the writer wants to tell us."
